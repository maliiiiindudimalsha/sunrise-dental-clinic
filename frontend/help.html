Help & Instructions

Step-by-step guidance for staff using the system.

How do I register a new appointment? +
Go to "Register Appointment". Enter the patient name, contact number and address, select a dentist and treatment, then choose the appointment date and time. Click "Register Appointment". The system automatically generates a unique appointment number.
How do I find or update an appointment? +
Go to "Search / Update" and select the required appointment from the appointment dropdown. The appointment details will load automatically. Patient details, date and time can then be updated, or the appointment can be cancelled.
How do I generate a bill? +
Go to "Billing & Receipt" and select an appointment from the dropdown. Click "Generate Bill". The system calculates the bill automatically and displays the receipt. Click "Print Receipt" to print it.
How do I manage dentists and treatments? (Admin only) +
Go to "Manage Dentists". Administrators can add, edit or delete dentists and treatments, and update consultation fees.
How do I view reports? (Admin only) +
Go to "Reports & Analytics". Select a From Date, To Date and optionally a dentist. Click "Generate" to view total appointments, total revenue, busiest dentist, most booked treatment and the appointment breakdown.
How do I send an appointment reminder? +
Go to "Notifications". Select an appointment, choose SMS or Email, review or edit the automatically generated reminder message, and click "Send Notification". The notification is simulated and stored in the notification log.
What happens if an appointment is cancelled? +
Cancelled appointments remain in the system for record keeping but cannot be edited. They are also excluded from normal billing and report calculations.
What do I do if the system shows a connection error? +
Confirm that the Java backend server is running and that MySQL is started in XAMPP. Then refresh the page and try again.
