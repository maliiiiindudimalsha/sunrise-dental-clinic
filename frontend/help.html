Help & Instructions

Step-by-step guidance for new staff using the system.

How do I register a new appointment? +
Go to "Register Appointment" in the sidebar. Fill in the patient's name, address, and contact number, choose a dentist and treatment type, then pick a date and time. Click "Register Appointment" - the system generates the appointment number automatically.
How do I find an existing appointment? +
Go to "Search / Update", type the appointment number (e.g. APT00001) into the search box, and click Search. You can then edit the patient's details or cancel the appointment from the same screen.
How do I generate a bill? +
Go to "Billing & Receipt", enter the appointment number, and click "Generate Bill". The total is calculated automatically from the treatment's consultation fee. Click "Print Receipt" to print it for the patient.
How do I add a new dentist or treatment? (Admin only) +
Go to "Manage Dentists" from the sidebar. Use the form under each table to add a new dentist or a new treatment with its consultation fee.
How do I view clinic reports? (Admin only) +
Go to "Reports & Analytics", choose a date range and optionally a dentist, then click "Generate". You'll see total appointments, revenue, and a breakdown table to help with decision-making.
What do I do if the system shows a connection error? +
This means the backend server isn't running. Ask your IT administrator to start the Java server and confirm XAMPP's MySQL service is running before trying again.
